I’m a Product Designer with 10+ years of turning messy problems into experiences that just make sense.

I studied Computer Science and, soon after, started my own company "KreativeJohnys", building custom ERPs. Somewhere between database schemas, client calls, and watching our designers turn complexity into something people could actually use, I realised I wanted to be on that side of problem-solving.

That’s how I found my way into User Experience Design.

Today, I lead design at Housing.com, where I work at the intersection of product strategy, systems thinking, and storytelling. I’m drawn to complex problems, and I believe good design should make them feel simple. I believe every screen should earn its place, and every interaction should feel intentional.

When I’m not designing, you’ll probably find me rotting on the couch completely guilt-free (not), filming beauty content for my socials (this is new), or waiting for the perfect opportunity to drop a “that’s what she said.” 😉
