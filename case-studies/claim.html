Claim Centre: an emotion-driven way to file a claim

Filing a life insurance claim happens at one of the hardest moments in someone's life. We turned a paper-heavy, agent-dependent process into a digital journey people could complete themselves.

The objective

A digital, intuitive claim journey that simplifies the process.

Emotion-driven journeys combined with just the essential information, so people are guided through a claim instead of buried in forms.

Success criteria

Goal 0120% online claims

03 Design

Every feature answers a pain we heard

Based on feedback over the wireframes, the final interface.

Simplified formsSolves: Long, irrelevant forms

A step-by-step claim

The claim broken into short, guided steps, starting with who you are and what happened.

Emotion-ledSolves: Confusing, technical language

An intuitive, empathetic flow

Copy and illustrations acknowledge the loss, while each step explains why it is needed.

BiometricSolves: Fraud and manual checks

Verifying the claimant

A selfie check and location capture give more data at filing time, reducing fraud.

Auto-saveSolves: Lost progress, starting over

Checkpoints that save progress

Every step auto-saves, so a closed page never means starting again.

Penny dropSolves: Incorrect documentation

Instant bank validation

A penny-drop check validates the account, removing a common source of rework.

OCRSolves: Branch visits for more documents

Validating documents as they upload

OCR checks a document the moment it is uploaded and flags errors in real time.

TrackingSolves: No idea if it was submitted

Track your claim

Claim status by phone number or claim ID, with each stage and what is next.

Follow-upSolves: No acknowledgement or timeline

Timely communication

Notifications keep claimants informed at every step, without them having to chase.
