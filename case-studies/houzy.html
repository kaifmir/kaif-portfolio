01 Design approach

The core challenge: giving an AI the right personality

Structured frameworks define an AI's interaction goals, personification, power dynamics, traits, tone and key behaviours. We adapted one for the proptech context.

Design dimensionHouzy's decisionRationale
Interaction goalsReduce decision paralysis, build trust, accelerate qualified discoveryNot transactional search, but conversational sense-making
Level of personificationMedium: warm but professional, named "Houzy"Medium personification builds trust while keeping appropriate power dynamics
Power dynamicsA collaborative guide, not an authoritative advisorThe buyer stays in control; the AI provides scaffolding, not prescription
ToneConversational expert: informed but approachableFinancial decisions need professional clarity; real estate also needs engagement
Key behavioursGraceful clarification when ambiguous; proactive nudges when users stallGraceful error handling stops users feeling like they're talking to a wall
02 Personality deep-dive

How Houzy speaks: designing for trust at scale

Personality card

Houzy's tone spectrum

A bot's tone should mirror the brand without sounding robotic, and the personality chosen shapes how trust forms.

Professional 70%Conversational 30%

Why: buying a home in India is high-involvement. Users expect competence, not chattiness, but rigid formality creates distance.

Expert 65%Peer 35%

Why: users need to feel they're talking to someone who knows more, without feeling condescended to.

Warm 60%Neutral 40%

Why: property search is emotional. Coldness alienates, but too much warmth feels manipulative to buyers wary of overselling.

Houzy's playbook

Never a dead end

When the AI can't help, it suggests an alternative, so users never feel they're talking to a wall.

Designed with the Product and LLM teams

Ambiguous query"I want something nice"
Houzy"Definitely! To get you your kind of nice: are you thinking investment property, or somewhere you'd actually live? And what's non-negotiable for you: location, budget or size?"
Out-of-scope request"Can you help me get a home loan?"
Houzy"I don't handle loans directly, but I can connect you with Housing.com's partner banks who offer pre-approved rates. Want me to set that up?"
Frustration cuesDetected via sentiment analysis
Houzy"Seems like I'm not nailing this. Want me to loop in a human agent who can dig deeper?"
03 Conversation mapping

Mapping every path, including the dead ends

Each request is mapped to the slots a user gives, so Houzy knows exactly what to ask next.

Example: "Show me the floor plan"

Houzy checks which of three slots the user filled, then resolves, clarifies or recovers.

P · ProjectL · LocationC · ConfigExact match → open itSeveral matches → let them pickNo anchor → ask, never stall
