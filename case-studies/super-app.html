The problem

A seller's day was spread across mQuote, mPro, mPower, VYMO, mSmart and more.

Each app had its own login and experience. Sellers re-entered the same details, juggled calendars, and waited for HO's Excel sheets to know where they stood.

The objective

Give every seller access to multiple services in one application.

Sellers in the field needed one place for self-enablement, adoption and a seamless experience.

What the Super App is

A one-stop solution to create, manage and track everything around the sales life cycle, policy servicing and retention, and governance.

SalesServicePerformanceCapabilityEmpowerment

02 Define

Mapping the whole job before designing a screen

The job to be done

When I start my day in the field, I want to see what needs doing and how I'm tracking, so I can act without switching apps.

Every pain we heard pointed here. This became the brief for everything that followed.

BRD

A business requirement document the whole team signed up to

Assumptions, dependencies, KPIs and flow charts for every journey, written alongside business and engineering so the build started from one shared definition.

04 Prototype

Every feature answers a pain we heard

Wireframes were tested with sellers, and the feedback shaped the final UI.

Fragmented tools→ Login, integrated workflows, lead-centred work
Stale data→ A real-time, centralised dashboard
Missed moments→ Performance-linked nudges, unified notifications
Business risk→ Lead work without hopping into VYMO
LoginSolves: Fragmented tools

Seamless login to every sales asset

Biometric login improves both security and convenience.

NudgesSolves: Missed moments

Performance-linked nudges

Personalised recommendations for the best next action for each role, based on their workflows and connected to performance and activity KPIs.

DashboardSolves: Stale data

A centralised dashboard

Sales, activity and policy-login KPIs at a glance. Supervisors can also track their team's performance.

IntegrationSolves: Fragmented tools

Integrated workflows for a seamless seller journey

From lead to sale, service and retention. Proposer and insured details auto-fill in quotes and policy login from the CRM, and every quote is saved against the lead.

WorkflowsSolves: Fragmented tools · Business risk

Workflows centred around leads

Portfolio, activity and next steps for every customer in one place, with smart actions based on rules defined around leads. No more hopping between mPower and VYMO.

NotificationsSolves: Missed moments

Unified notifications

Notifications from multiple applications in one place, grouped by workflow, with a distinct sound for Super App push notifications.

The bigger goal

One Super App, fewer apps to run

The pilot was step one. The long-term goal is a single Super App for every seller, so the business can retire overlapping apps and cut the cost of running many of them.
